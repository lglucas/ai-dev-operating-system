# Business Plan Impact Review Skill

Use when revising BP v0.0.1 into v0.0.2 after user feedback.

Every change must be checked for downstream impact.

Examples: pricing affects projections; persona affects GTM and UX; stack affects costs and architecture; MVP affects roadmap and DoD.
